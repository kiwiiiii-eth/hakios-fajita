# HakiOS on the OnePlus 6T — Beta 0.4 (`v0.4-beta`)

November 2026. Kernel: [`kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-xnu-kernel`](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-xnu-kernel/tree/fajita-v0.4-beta),
tag `fajita-v0.4-beta`. Device: this repository, tag `v0.4-beta`.

Still a developer bring-up, built on your Mac with your own IPSW and your own phone's firmware; no Apple software and no
vendor firmware are distributed here. New: it can now be **installed**, so that the phone starts iOS by itself.

## What is new since Beta 0.3

- **Installed, the phone starts iOS from its power button** — no Mac, no cable (`./install.sh`). HakiOS goes into the
  slot Android is not using; Android stays whole in its own (`./switch.sh android` / `./switch.sh hakios`).
  - A partition `darwin` is made of the end of userdata (32 GiB by default) for the system image and `/private/var`.
    **This erases Android's data**: you type `ERASE` first. The loader writes the new partition table only if nothing
    but userdata's end and darwin changes (every other entry, the header and both copies are checked against the
    phone's), reads it back, and mends the two copies if a write was ever cut short; the rules were tried on 23 tables
    made from a real 6T's before any phone was written.
  - Everything changed is backed up first, in `~/HakiOS-6T-backup/<serial>/`; `./uninstall.sh` puts it all back.
  - The boot partition holds HakiOS's U-Boot and loader; the loader reads the system image from darwin (about
    17 seconds), marks the slot as started (as Android does), and starts the kernel.
- **Settings and data are kept**: what iOS keeps in `/private/var` (Wi-Fi networks, settings, app data) is saved into
  darwin as iOS shuts down or restarts (about a minute), and is there at the next start. Shut down and Restart in iOS
  now really power the phone off or restart it.
- **The time is right without a Mac**: the PMIC's real-time clock keeps counting while the phone is off, and the offset
  to the date is kept, so a phone started on its own begins at the right time. (The count was seen to start again from
  zero twice, after the phone was got out of a stuck loader: the clock is then left as the image has it rather than set
  back, until iOS has the network's time.)
- **The time zone from the cellular network** (NITZ), as an iPhone's "Set Automatically" does: in Taiwan, Asia/Taipei.
- **The carrier's name shows** in the status bar (it was four boxes: PingFang's Medium weight was not in the image).
- Runs from the Mac (`./run.sh`) last an hour by default, and the phone goes back to fastboot when one ends (iOS's last
  picture used to stay on the screen and looked like a phone whose touch had died).

## Known issues

- Settings → General → About shows about 1 GB: `/private/var` is a memory filesystem saved into darwin, not yet a volume
  of its own (next release).
- `./uninstall.sh` makes userdata again by default (Android's data erased once more); `KEEP_ANDROID_DATA=1` skips it,
  and the space comes back only if Android grows into it by itself.
- With `./run.sh` nothing on the phone survives a reboot (install for that). Bluetooth pairings are lost at every boot.
- Scrolling is not as smooth as an iPhone's; no cameras; `neofetch` says "iPhone X / A11".
- Some routers isolate their clients: if your Mac cannot reach the phone over Wi-Fi, put both on an iPhone's hotspot.
  An installed phone prefers a Wi-Fi network it knows over a hotspot, as iOS does.

# 繁體中文

仍是開發者移植，用你自己的 IPSW 和你自己手機的韌體在 Mac 上建置；這裡不散布任何 Apple 軟體或原廠韌體。這一版的重點：**可以安裝到手機**，按電源鍵直接開進 iOS。

**新增：**
- **安裝到手機，按電源鍵就進 iOS**，不用 Mac、不用接線（`./install.sh`）。HakiOS 裝進 Android 沒在用的 slot，Android 原封不動留在自己的 slot（`./switch.sh android`／`./switch.sh hakios` 切換）。
  - 從 userdata 尾端切出 `darwin` 分區（預設 32 GiB），放系統映像與 `/private/var`。**會清除 Android 的資料**，要先輸入 `ERASE`。
  - loader 只在「只改 userdata 結尾和 darwin」時才寫分區表：其他每個 entry、表頭、兩份副本都要和手機上的一致；寫完讀回比對，若曾寫到一半中斷也能修好。這套規則在寫任何手機之前，先用真實 6T 分區表做出的 23 種情況測過。
  - 動到的東西都先備份到 `~/HakiOS-6T-backup/<序號>/`，`./uninstall.sh` 會全部還原。
- **設定與資料會保留**：Wi‑Fi、設定、App 資料（`/private/var`）在 iOS 關機或重開時存進 darwin（約 1 分鐘），下次開機都還在。iOS 的關機、重新開機現在會真的斷電或重開。
- **不接 Mac 時間也正確**：PMIC 的 RTC 關機時仍在計數，配合保存的差值，開機就是正確時間。（RTC 曾兩次被歸零，都在從卡住的 loader 救回手機時；這時不會把時鐘往回撥，等 iOS 從網路取得時間。）
- **時區跟著行動網路自動設定**（NITZ），和 iPhone 的「自動設定」一樣：在台灣是 Asia/Taipei。
- **狀態列的電信商名稱正常顯示**（原本是四個方塊：映像裡少了蘋方的 Medium 字重）。
- 從 Mac 開機（`./run.sh`）預設一輪一小時，結束後手機自動回到 fastboot 畫面。

**已知問題：**
- 「設定 → 一般 → 關於本機」容量只顯示約 1 GB（下一版處理）。
- `./uninstall.sh` 預設會重建 userdata（Android 資料再清一次）；`KEEP_ANDROID_DATA=1` 可略過，但空間要靠 Android 自己長回來。
- 用 `./run.sh` 時資料不會保留（要保留請安裝）；藍牙配對每次開機都要重來。
- 滑動還沒有 iPhone 順；沒有相機；`neofetch` 顯示 iPhone X／A11。
- 部分路由器會隔離用戶端，Mac 連不到手機時讓兩台都連 iPhone 熱點。安裝後的手機會優先連已知的 Wi‑Fi，而不是熱點（和 iOS 一樣）。
