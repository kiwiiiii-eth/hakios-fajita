# Beta 0.7.1 — every recording has sound

**HakiOS for the OnePlus 6T** runs the Darwin (XNU) kernel and the iOS 16.5 user space on a OnePlus 6T. Installed, the phone starts iOS from its power button; Android stays in its other slot. It is still a developer bring-up: you build it on your Mac from your own IPSW and your own phone's firmware. No Apple software and no vendor firmware are distributed here. [中文說明在下方](#繁體中文)

A fix release on top of [Beta 0.7](v0.7-beta.md): nothing new, three fixes.

## Fixed

- **Every recording after the first of a start was silent** ([#2](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-fajita/issues/2)): Voice Memos, and any app that records. The microphone's SLIMbus device keeps its logical address between two recordings, and the driver waited for a handshake that only a new address brings; it gave up, and after three tries the microphone was off until a restart. A device that still has its address is now used as it is. Reported by @tre4surehunter9, thank you!
- **A panic at shutdown or restart while Wi‑Fi was scanning**: the station's calls are drained and the driver's timers stopped before the driver is taken down.
- **Chinese** (Traditional, Hong Kong, Simplified) is kept in the system image: Settings → General → Language & Region now switches iOS to Chinese. Before, the languages' files were left out and the switch changed nothing.

## Known issues

The same as Beta 0.7, and two more:

- **Joining a saved Wi‑Fi network was not tested on this build**: no saved network was in range during the test (scanning was, and works). Nothing in this release changes how Wi‑Fi joins.
- **After an update from an earlier Beta**, the home screen may keep the apps' names in English once iOS is switched to Chinese (Settings and the apps themselves are in Chinese): the names were recorded at the first start, when the system had no Chinese.
- **The AirPods card's animation** stays black; the card works.
- **Videos in Safari** (YouTube) may not play.
- **iOS downloads system assets** (a few MB to tens of MB); without Wi‑Fi this goes over the cellular data.
- **Wi‑Fi** does not join an access point that offers only 802.11g (no HT).
- **SMS** received while HakiOS runs are not kept; no calls; no App Store or Apple ID.

## Install or update

| Beta 0.4 to 0.7 installed | `FW=<the old kit>/work/hakios-xnu-kernel ./setup.sh <your IPSW>` with the new kit, then `./install.sh` again (`ISLAND=1` for the island): nothing is erased |

# 繁體中文

**HakiOS for OnePlus 6T** 讓 OnePlus 6T 跑 Darwin（XNU）核心與 iOS 16.5 使用者空間。安裝後按電源鍵就進 iOS，Android 留在另一個 slot。這仍是開發中的移植：要在自己的 Mac 上用自己的 IPSW 和自己手機的韌體建置，這裡不散布任何 Apple 軟體或廠商韌體。

以 [Beta 0.7](v0.7-beta.md#繁體中文) 為基礎的修正版：沒有新功能，三項修正。

## 修正

- **開機後第一次以後的錄音都沒聲音**（[#2](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-fajita/issues/2)）：語音備忘錄和所有會錄音的 App 都會。麥克風的 SLIMbus 裝置在兩次錄音之間會保留邏輯位址，驅動卻在等只有新位址才會有的交握，等不到就放棄，連三次後麥克風整個關掉，要重開機才回來。現在保留位址的裝置直接沿用。由 @tre4surehunter9 回報，謝謝！
- **Wi‑Fi 還在掃描時關機或重開會當機**：卸下驅動前先讓基地台相關的呼叫結束，並停掉驅動的計時器。
- 系統映像保留**中文**（繁體、香港、簡體）：「設定 → 一般 → 語言與地區」現在能把 iOS 切成中文。以前語言檔沒放進去，切了沒反應。

## 已知問題

和 Beta 0.7 相同，另外多兩項：

- **這個 build 沒有實測連上已儲存的 Wi‑Fi**：測試時附近沒有存過的網路（有測掃描，正常）。這版沒有改到 Wi‑Fi 的連線方式。
- **從舊版 Beta 更新的話**，iOS 切成中文後，主畫面的 App 名稱可能還是英文（設定和 App 裡面會是中文）：名稱是第一次開機時記下的，那時系統還沒有中文。
- **AirPods 卡片的動畫**是黑的，卡片本身能用。
- **Safari 裡的影片**（YouTube）可能播不出來。
- **iOS 會下載系統素材**（幾 MB 到幾十 MB），沒有 Wi‑Fi 時會用行動數據。
- **Wi‑Fi** 連不上只支援 802.11g（沒有 HT）的基地台。
- **簡訊**：HakiOS 執行時收到的不會保存；不能打電話；不能用 App Store、Apple ID。
