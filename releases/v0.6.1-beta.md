# Beta 0.6.1 — the Dynamic Island, fixed

**HakiOS for the OnePlus 6T** runs the Darwin (XNU) kernel and the iOS 16.5 user space on a OnePlus 6T. Installed, the phone starts iOS from its power button; Android stays in its other slot. It is still a developer bring-up: you build it on your Mac from your own IPSW and your own phone's firmware. No Apple software and no vendor firmware are distributed here. [中文說明在下方](#繁體中文)

## Highlights

- 🏝 **The Dynamic Island works as on an iPhone 14 Pro** (`ISLAND=1 ./install.sh`): the island at the top, the time to its left and the battery to its right, and the swipe up to go home. In 0.6 it laid the phone out as one with a home button.
- 🛠 **Every install now tells iOS the phone has no home button.** It used to hold only through an answer iOS had cached; once that cache was rebuilt, the same breakage could reach the normal (notch) layout too.
- ✉️ **SMS, experimental** (`EXTRA="tel=1"`): the modem's text messages are read and written to the log as they come. Nothing is sent, and Messages does not show them yet.

## What's new

### Fixed
- **Dynamic Island** (`ISLAND=1`): no gestures, the old 20-point status bar, and the island over the first row of icons. iOS took the phone for one with a home button: the device tree had no `/buttons` node, so MobileGestalt's HomeButtonType was 0, and SpringBoard looks up the 14 Pro's screen only among phones without one. The loader now gives `/buttons home-button-type` 2, as on the iPhone X and the 14 Pro.
- With `island=1` the kernel reports the 14 Pro's screen, 1179 x 2556 (393 x 852 points), scaled to the panel's 1080 x 2340 (the same shape), because SpringBoard picks its screen type by size. `fbwidth`/`fbheight` still override it.
- The cached MobileGestalt answers are dropped when the island or the screen's size changes, and once more with this release, so a phone that ran 0.6 with the island comes back right at its first start.

### Added
- `tel=1` (a kernel argument, off by default): calls and SMS are asked of the modem, read only. On LTE with a combined attach, SMS arrive over SGs; each one is logged with only the sender's last three digits. Calls need VoLTE, and the modem's IMS does not run here (Taiwan has no 3G left), so there are no calls.

## Known issues

- **SMS that arrive while HakiOS runs are not kept**: the modem hands each message over instead of storing it, and nothing in iOS takes it yet. Use Android for messages you need.
- **The Dynamic Island draws about 10% more pixels** than the notch layout (the 14 Pro's screen); scrolling may be a little less smooth with it.
- **Wi‑Fi** does not join an access point that offers only 802.11g (no HT); access points with 802.11n or newer join as before.
- **Video** records with sound but has not been checked closely; the second rear camera is not used.
- **Apple's services** need an activated device (App Store, Apple ID, Apple Maps); Bluetooth pairings are not yet confirmed to survive a restart; no calls; `neofetch` says "iPhone X / A11".

# 繁體中文

**HakiOS for OnePlus 6T** 讓 OnePlus 6T 跑 Darwin（XNU）核心＋iOS 16.5 使用者空間。安裝後按電源鍵直接進 iOS，Android 留在另一個 slot。目前仍是開發者移植：用你自己的 IPSW 和你自己手機的韌體在 Mac 上建置，這裡不散布任何 Apple 軟體或原廠韌體。

## 重點

- 🏝 **動態島跟 iPhone 14 Pro 一樣了**（`ISLAND=1 ./install.sh`）：島在最上方，時間在左、電池在右，可以上滑回主畫面。0.6 會被當成有 Home 鍵的機型、整個跑版。
- 🛠 **每一種安裝都會告訴 iOS「沒有 Home 鍵」**：以前只是靠 iOS 快取裡的舊答案撐著，快取一旦重建，一般（瀏海）版面也可能出同樣的問題。
- ✉️ **簡訊（實驗中，`EXTRA="tel=1"`）**：數據機收到的簡訊會寫進記錄。不會發出任何東西，「訊息」App 還看不到。

## 更新內容

### 修正
- **動態島**（`ISLAND=1`）：原本沒有手勢、狀態列是舊式 20 pt、島壓在第一排圖示上。原因是 iOS 把手機當成有 Home 鍵的機型：device tree 沒有 `/buttons` 節點，MobileGestalt 的 HomeButtonType 是 0，而 SpringBoard 只會在「沒有 Home 鍵」的機型裡找 14 Pro 的螢幕。現在 loader 會給 `/buttons home-button-type` 2，跟 iPhone X、14 Pro 一樣。
- 開 `island=1` 時，核心會回報 14 Pro 的螢幕 1179 x 2556（393 x 852 點），再縮放到面板的 1080 x 2340（長寬比相同），因為 SpringBoard 是依尺寸判斷螢幕類型。`fbwidth`／`fbheight` 仍可覆寫。
- 動態島開關或螢幕尺寸改變時，會清掉 MobileGestalt 的快取；這一版也會再清一次，所以用 0.6 開過動態島的手機，第一次開機就會恢復正常。

### 新增
- `tel=1`（核心參數，預設關）：向數據機查詢通話與簡訊狀態，只讀不改。LTE 聯合附著時簡訊走 SGs 進來，每則都會寫進記錄（寄件者只留末 3 碼）。打電話需要 VoLTE，這裡數據機的 IMS 沒有在跑，台灣也沒有 3G 了，所以不能打電話。

## 已知問題

- **HakiOS 執行時收到的簡訊不會保留**：數據機收到後直接轉交、不儲存，而 iOS 這邊還沒有程式接收。重要簡訊請用 Android 收。
- **動態島版要多畫約 10% 的像素**（14 Pro 的螢幕），滑動可能稍微不順一點。
- **Wi‑Fi** 連不上只支援 802.11g（沒有 HT）的基地台；支援 802.11n 以上的照常連得上。
- **錄影**有聲音，但還沒仔細檢查；第二顆後鏡頭還沒用上。
- **Apple 服務**需要已啟用的裝置（App Store、Apple ID、Apple 地圖）；藍牙配對重開後是否保留還沒確認；不能打電話；`neofetch` 顯示 iPhone X／A11。
