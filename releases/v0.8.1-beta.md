# Beta 0.8.1 — Personal Hotspot works

**HakiOS for the OnePlus 6T** runs the Darwin (XNU) kernel and the iOS 16.5 user space on a OnePlus 6T. Installed, the phone starts iOS from its power button; Android stays in its other slot. It is still a developer bring-up: you build it on your Mac from your own IPSW and your own phone's firmware. No Apple software and no vendor firmware are distributed here. [中文說明在下方](#繁體中文)

A fix release on top of [Beta 0.8](v0.8-beta.md): the Personal Hotspot, which 0.8 shipped as "leave it off", works, and is turned on and shown as on an iPhone.

## Fixed

- **A device that left the hotspot could not join again** (until a restart), and one that came back got no traffic. When a station left, the Wi‑Fi layer took it down one step at a time and stopped at the first step the driver refused, so the last one, which deletes the firmware's peer, was never done: the firmware kept the old peer and would not make it again. Every step down is now taken, as mac80211 does, and the station's key is freed only once the driver is done with it (it read zeroes before: "cipher 0 is not supported"). Tested with an ESP32 client joining and leaving three ways (a disconnect, a join given up halfway, the radio switched off) in a loop: web, DNS and UDP through the hotspot every time.
- **The hotspot came up at every start** if it was on before. It is off at each start now, and is turned on in Settings → Personal Hotspot (Allow Others to Join) or in Control Center.

## Added

- **Maximize Compatibility** in the Personal Hotspot pane, in iOS's own words: on, the hotspot is on 2.4 GHz (channel 6); off, on 5 GHz (channel 149) where the radio may transmit first, and back on 2.4 GHz if it cannot start there.
- **The green Personal Hotspot icon** in the Dynamic Island (the status bar) while devices are joined: SpringBoard's own, told how many are joined (`kern.hakios.hotspot_stations`).
- **Control Center's Personal Hotspot button** (press and hold the connectivity block): "Discoverable" or the number of connections, and a tap turns it on or off; Settings' switch follows.

## Known issues

- **Devices on your hotspot use your cellular data plan.**
- The hotspot runs **802.11g/a** (20 MHz, no 802.11n): up to 54 Mbps, slower than a real iPhone's.
- A device that gives up joining halfway and tries again at once may wait **30–45 seconds** to get in.
- **Cellular data** is tested only with FarEasTone (Taiwan); roaming is not tested. Read [Beta 0.8's notes](v0.8-beta.md#cellular-data-before-you-use-it) before you use it.
- **No calls**; SMS received while HakiOS runs are not kept; no App Store or Apple ID.
- **After an update from an earlier Beta**, the home screen may keep the apps' names in English once iOS is switched to Chinese.
- **The AirPods card's animation** stays black; **videos in Safari** (YouTube) may not play; **Wi‑Fi** does not join an access point that offers only 802.11g.

# 繁體中文

**HakiOS for OnePlus 6T** 讓 OnePlus 6T 跑 Darwin（XNU）核心與 iOS 16.5 使用者空間。安裝後按電源鍵就進 iOS，Android 留在另一個 slot。這仍是開發中的移植：要在自己的 Mac 上用自己的 IPSW 和自己手機的韌體建置，這裡不散布任何 Apple 軟體或廠商韌體。

以 [Beta 0.8](v0.8-beta.md#繁體中文) 為基礎的修正版：0.8 寫著「先不要打開」的個人熱點現在能用了，開法和顯示都跟 iPhone 一樣。

## 修正

- **裝置離開熱點後連不回來**（要重開機），連回來的也沒有流量。裝置離開時，Wi‑Fi 層一步步把它拆掉，但只要驅動拒絕其中一步就停下，所以最後一步「刪除韌體裡的連線對象」從沒做到：韌體留著舊的，也不肯再建一次。現在照 mac80211 的做法每一步都做到底，裝置的金鑰也等驅動用完才釋放（之前會讀到被清空的資料：「cipher 0 is not supported」）。用 ESP32 反覆以三種方式（正常斷線、連到一半放棄、直接關無線電）加入、離開測試，每次都能透過熱點上網、查 DNS、走 UDP。
- **之前開過熱點，開機時熱點就會自己打開**。現在每次開機都是關的，要從「設定 → 個人熱點」打開「允許其他人加入」，或從控制中心打開。

## 新增

- 個人熱點頁的**最大化相容性**（iOS 原本的文字）：開啟時熱點用 2.4 GHz（頻道 6）；關閉時用 5 GHz（頻道 149，限無線晶片可主動發射的地方），開不起來就退回 2.4 GHz。
- 有裝置連上時，**動態島（狀態列）出現綠色個人熱點圖示**：用 SpringBoard 自己的顯示，由我們告訴它目前幾台連著（`kern.hakios.hotspot_stations`）。
- **控制中心的「個人熱點」按鈕**（長按連線區塊）：顯示「可被搜尋」或連線數，點一下就能開關，「設定」裡的開關會跟著變。

## 已知問題

- **連上你熱點的裝置會用到你的行動數據流量。**
- 熱點是 **802.11g/a**（20 MHz、沒有 802.11n）：最快 54 Mbps，比真的 iPhone 熱點慢。
- 裝置連到一半放棄又馬上重連的話，可能要等 **30～45 秒**才連得上。
- **行動數據**只在遠傳（台灣）實測過，漫遊沒測過，使用前請先看 [Beta 0.8 的說明](v0.8-beta.md#行動數據使用前請先看)。
- **不能打電話**；HakiOS 執行時收到的簡訊不會保存；不能用 App Store、Apple ID。
- **從舊版 Beta 更新的話**，iOS 切成中文後，主畫面的 App 名稱可能還是英文。
- **AirPods 卡片的動畫**是黑的；**Safari 影片**（YouTube）可能播不出來；**Wi‑Fi** 連不上只支援 802.11g 的基地台。
