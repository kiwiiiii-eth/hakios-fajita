# Beta 0.8 — cellular data the iPhone's way

**HakiOS for the OnePlus 6T** runs the Darwin (XNU) kernel and the iOS 16.5 user space on a OnePlus 6T. Installed, the phone starts iOS from its power button; Android stays in its other slot. It is still a developer bring-up: you build it on your Mac from your own IPSW and your own phone's firmware. No Apple software and no vendor firmware are distributed here. [中文說明在下方](#繁體中文)

## Highlights

- 📶 **Cellular data through iOS itself**: CommCenter, iOS's cellular service, now runs the 6T's modem. Settings → Cellular is iOS's own page (the Cellular Data switch, a switch per app, the data used, the carrier); IPv4 and IPv6.
- 🏷️ **The carrier's settings** come from iOS's carrier bundles, kept in the system image that `setup.sh` builds from your IPSW.
- ⏻ **Power menu** (power + volume down) and the **volume indicator** beside the 6T's buttons.
- ⚠️ **Read [Cellular data: before you use it](#cellular-data-before-you-use-it)**: tested with one carrier only, and it uses your data plan.

## What's new

### Added
- **Cellular data through CommCenter** (`bbdt=1`, `abb=1`, `modem_native_pdp=1`, all on by default). Up to 0.7 the kernel opened a data session of its own on the modem (`en1`) and CommCenter was kept from running. Now CommCenter and its baseband manager run on the Qualcomm modem: the loader adds a `/baseband` node to the device tree (launchd starts the cellular CommCenter, MobileGestalt reports cellular); the kernel answers as AppleBaseband, AppleIPAppender and the ACIPC drivers would, and bridges CommCenter's QMI (QMUX) to the modem's QRTR (`/dev/hakios-qmuxN`); CommCenter's data calls get native `pdp_ip0`…`pdp_ip15` interfaces over the IPA. The kernel opens no session of its own, and keeps the modem's data setting on. In CommCenter (`libhakios.cc.dylib`), the modem counts as provisioned once it is registered, and the baseband reset that CommCenter asks for after loading a carrier bundle, which this modem cannot take, is skipped. Measured: IPv4 and IPv6 answer within two minutes of the start and through a 30-minute session; Cellular Data off cuts the data, and on brings it back in about 40 seconds.
- **Carrier bundles** (39 MB of your IPSW) are kept in the system image: CommCenter finds the SIM's carrier there, with its APNs, its name, and whether data is allowed.
- **Settings**: Cellular opens iOS's own pane; Location Services is back in Privacy; a Personal Hotspot row and pane (experimental: see the known issues).
- **SpringBoard**: power and volume down held together for 1.2 s bring up iOS's "slide to power off"; the volume indicator is drawn beside the 6T's volume buttons, which sit lower than an iPhone's (`hud_button_offset=POINTS` moves it).
- **Personal Hotspot, experimental**: the Wi‑Fi radio as a WPA2 access point, with DHCP and NAT out of the cellular interface. Devices join; their traffic does not get through reliably yet.

### Changed
- `setup.sh` lets CommCenter run (`ALLOW="locationd lockdown CommCenter"`).

## Cellular data: before you use it

- **Tested only with FarEasTone (Taiwan)**, one SIM, in Taiwan. Other carriers and countries have not been tried: iOS picks the carrier's settings from your IPSW's carrier bundles, and a carrier it has no bundle for may get no data. If data does not connect, open an [issue](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-fajita/issues) with the carrier's name, the country and the log.
- **It uses your data plan.** Without Wi‑Fi, iOS and the apps go over the cellular data, and iOS downloads system assets in the background (a few MB to tens of MB). Settings → Cellular shows the data used and turns it off for one app or for all.
- **Roaming has not been tested.** Turn Cellular Data off before travelling abroad.
- After Cellular Data is turned on again, it takes about 40 seconds to connect.
- **No calls**, SMS received while HakiOS runs are not kept, no MMS.
- A phone without a SIM card has not been tried on this build.

## Known issues

- **Personal Hotspot does not work yet.** It is in Settings, and other devices see it and join, but they get no internet; after a device leaves, the Wi‑Fi firmware stops answering for it, and it may not join again until a restart. Leave it off.
- **After an update from an earlier Beta**, the home screen may keep the apps' names in English once iOS is switched to Chinese (Settings and the apps themselves are in Chinese).
- **The AirPods card's animation** stays black; the card works.
- **Videos in Safari** (YouTube) may not play.
- **Wi‑Fi** does not join an access point that offers only 802.11g (no HT).
- No App Store or Apple ID.

# 繁體中文

**HakiOS for OnePlus 6T** 讓 OnePlus 6T 跑 Darwin（XNU）核心與 iOS 16.5 使用者空間。安裝後按電源鍵就進 iOS，Android 留在另一個 slot。這仍是開發中的移植：要在自己的 Mac 上用自己的 IPSW 和自己手機的韌體建置，這裡不散布任何 Apple 軟體或廠商韌體。

## 重點

- 📶 **行動數據由 iOS 自己管理**：iOS 的行動服務 CommCenter 現在直接控制 6T 的數據機。「設定 → 行動服務」是 iOS 原本的頁面（行動數據開關、每個 App 的開關、用量、電信商），支援 IPv4 與 IPv6。
- 🏷️ **電信商設定**來自 iOS 的電信商設定檔，放在 `setup.sh` 用你的 IPSW 建的系統映像裡。
- ⏻ **關機選單**（電源鍵＋音量下鍵），**音量指示**改到 6T 的按鍵旁邊。
- ⚠️ **請先看[行動數據：使用前請先看](#行動數據使用前請先看)**：只測過一家電信商，而且會用到你的數據流量。

## 新內容

### 新增
- **行動數據改走 CommCenter**（`bbdt=1`、`abb=1`、`modem_native_pdp=1`，都預設開啟）。到 0.7 為止是核心自己在數據機上開數據連線（`en1`），CommCenter 被擋著不跑。現在 CommCenter 和它的基頻管理程式直接跑在 Qualcomm 數據機上：loader 在 device tree 加上 `/baseband` 節點（launchd 會啟動有行動網路的 CommCenter，MobileGestalt 回報有行動網路）；核心扮演 AppleBaseband、AppleIPAppender 和 ACIPC 驅動，並把 CommCenter 的 QMI（QMUX）轉給數據機的 QRTR（`/dev/hakios-qmuxN`）；CommCenter 的數據連線走原生的 `pdp_ip0`～`pdp_ip15` 介面，經過 IPA。核心不再自己開連線，只負責讓數據機的數據開關保持開啟。CommCenter 裡（`libhakios.cc.dylib`）：數據機只要註冊上網路就算開通；CommCenter 載入電信商設定檔後會要求重置基頻，這顆數據機承受不了，所以略過。實測：開機兩分鐘內 IPv4 與 IPv6 都能連，連續 30 分鐘正常；關掉行動數據會斷線，打開後約 40 秒恢復。
- **電信商設定檔**（從你的 IPSW 取出，39 MB）放進系統映像：CommCenter 從這裡找到 SIM 卡的電信商，包括 APN、名稱，以及是否允許數據。
- **設定**：「行動服務」打開的是 iOS 原本的頁面；「隱私權」裡的定位服務回來了；新增個人熱點的列與頁面（實驗中，見已知問題）。
- **SpringBoard**：同時按住電源鍵和音量下鍵 1.2 秒，出現 iOS 的「滑動來關機」；音量指示畫在 6T 的音量鍵旁（6T 的按鍵比 iPhone 低，`hud_button_offset=點數` 可調）。
- **個人熱點（實驗中）**：Wi‑Fi 晶片當 WPA2 基地台，配 DHCP 和從行動網路出去的 NAT。裝置連得上，但流量還不能穩定通過。

### 改進
- `setup.sh` 讓 CommCenter 執行（`ALLOW="locationd lockdown CommCenter"`）。

## 行動數據：使用前請先看

- **只在遠傳電信（台灣）實測過**，一張 SIM 卡、在台灣。其他電信商和國家都還沒試過：iOS 依你 IPSW 裡的電信商設定檔選設定，沒有設定檔的電信商可能上不了網。連不上的話，請到 [issues](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-fajita/issues) 回報，附上電信商名稱、國家和記錄檔。
- **會用到你的數據流量。** 沒有 Wi‑Fi 時，iOS 和 App 都走行動數據，iOS 也會在背景下載系統素材（幾 MB 到幾十 MB）。「設定 → 行動服務」看得到用量，也能關掉單一 App 或全部的行動數據。
- **漫遊沒有測過。** 出國前請先關掉行動數據。
- 行動數據關掉再打開後，大約 40 秒才會重新連上。
- **不能打電話**，HakiOS 執行時收到的簡訊不會保存，沒有 MMS。
- 這個版本沒有測過不插 SIM 卡的情況。

## 已知問題

- **個人熱點還不能用。** 設定裡有，其他裝置看得到也連得上，但上不了網；裝置離開後 Wi‑Fi 韌體就不再回應它，可能要重開機才連得回來。請先不要打開。
- **從舊版 Beta 更新的話**，iOS 切成中文後，主畫面的 App 名稱可能還是英文（設定和 App 裡面會是中文）。
- **AirPods 卡片的動畫**是黑的，卡片本身能用。
- **Safari 裡的影片**（YouTube）可能播不出來。
- **Wi‑Fi** 連不上只支援 802.11g（沒有 HT）的基地台。
- 不能用 App Store、Apple ID。
