# HakiOS on the OnePlus 6T — Beta 0.1 (`v0.1-beta`)

November 2026. Kernel: [`kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-xnu-kernel`](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-xnu-kernel/tree/fajita-v0.1-beta),
tag `fajita-v0.1-beta`. Device: this repository, tag `v0.1-beta`.

The first version meant for other people to try. It is still a developer bring-up: tethered to a Mac over USB, the whole
system in RAM (nothing is flashed; a reboot goes back to Android), and you build it from source with your own IPSW and
your own phone's firmware. No Apple software and no vendor firmware are distributed here.

## What is new since the first public push

- **Sound from the speaker** (on by default): the audio DSP, the QUAT MI2S interface and the TFA9894 amplifier, driven
  over I2C by hand in bypass mode. iOS's sounds, keyboard clicks and YouTube play, at Darwin's volume. Every sample is
  limited to −20 dBFS and the amplifier's status is read every 200 ms (any fault powers it down for the rest of the run).
- **Ringtones** in the system image (`Library/Ringtones`), which Settings' ringtone list needs (not yet tried on the phone).
- **Bluetooth** (`bt=4`): the WCN3990 over GENI UART6 with Qualcomm's rampatch and NVM; iOS turns Bluetooth on, scans,
  and pairs with a Mac. A keychain (`aks_keys=1`) keeps pairings within a run.
- **SSH**: `sshd` with your own key, and `neofetch` preinstalled and shown at login.
- **LTE data** (`ipa8=11`), with the status bar's carrier name, bars and 4G.
- Fixes: the kernel's I/O map grown from 8 to 128 MB (permanent register mappings ran out: a panic), a panic's text saved
  before the hardware is quiesced, the modem stopped in order when a run ends.

## Known issues

- BLE keyboards pair but do not type; a pairing does not survive leaving Bluetooth's page; Control Center shows Bluetooth off.
- No microphone, no cameras, no persistent storage (`/private/var` is made afresh at every boot).
- Wi-Fi networks are joined again by hand after every boot.
- Some routers isolate their clients: if your Mac cannot reach the phone over Wi-Fi, put both on an iPhone's hotspot.
- `neofetch` says "iPhone X / A11": the user space believes it is an iPhone10,3.
- Rarely, a run stops at boot with `initproc failed to start` (programs killed by SIGBUS as the modem starts; once in
  130 runs, cause not found yet): run it again.
- `pull-firmware.sh` (firmware over adb) is new and was tried against a stand-in for the phone only: report how it goes.

## Testing

Download `hakios-6t-v0.1-beta.tar.gz` below, then see [Testing Beta 0.1](../README.md#testing-beta-01) or the kit's own README. When something breaks, open an issue with the run's log

# 繁體中文

第一個給別人試用的版本，但仍是開發者移植：

- 要用 USB 接著 Mac 開機。
- 整套系統跑在記憶體裡，不刷任何分區，重開機就回到 Android。
- 要用你自己的 IPSW 和你自己手機的韌體從原始碼建置。
- 這裡不散布任何 Apple 軟體或原廠韌體。

**新增：**
- **喇叭出聲**（預設開啟）：iOS 系統音、鍵盤音、YouTube 都會從喇叭出來，跟著 iOS 的音量。
  - 為保護喇叭，聲音一律限制在 −20 dBFS。
  - 功放每 200 ms 檢查一次狀態，有故障就整輪關閉。
- **內建鈴聲**：補進系統映像，設定裡的鈴聲頁需要它（還沒上機實測）。
- **藍牙**（`bt=4`）：iOS 能開藍牙、掃描、和 Mac 配對。
- **SSH**：只收你自己的金鑰，預裝 `neofetch`，登入就顯示。
- **LTE 行動數據**（`ipa8=11`）：狀態列顯示電信名稱、訊號格數和 4G。
- **修正**：核心 I/O 映射從 8 MB 加大到 128 MB（原本不夠用會當機）；當機訊息會先存下來；結束時依序關閉數據機。

**已知問題：**
- BLE 鍵盤能配對但不能打字；離開藍牙頁後配對會消失；控制中心的藍牙顯示關閉。
- 沒有麥克風、相機，也不能保存資料（重開機後清空）。
- 每次開機都要重新加入 Wi‑Fi。
- 部分路由器會隔離用戶端。Mac 連不到手機時，讓兩台都連 iPhone 熱點。
- `neofetch` 會顯示 iPhone X／A11，因為系統以為自己是 iPhone X。
- 偶爾開機會停在 `initproc failed to start`（數據機啟動瞬間程式被 SIGBUS 殺掉，130 多輪出現一次，原因未明）：再跑一次即可。
- `pull-firmware.sh`（用 adb 抽韌體）是新寫的，只用模擬手機測過，請回報實際情況。

**測試方式：**下載下方的 `hakios-6t-v0.1-beta.tar.gz`，再看 [README 的「測試 Beta 0.1」](../README.zh-TW.md#測試-beta-01)或測試包裡的 README。

**回報問題：**請附上那一輪的紀錄檔（`out/logs/<名稱>.txt`）、你做了什麼、看到什麼。
