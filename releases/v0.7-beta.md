# Beta 0.7 — AirPods, smoother scrolling, pairings that stay

**HakiOS for the OnePlus 6T** runs the Darwin (XNU) kernel and the iOS 16.5 user space on a OnePlus 6T. Installed, the phone starts iOS from its power button; Android stays in its other slot. It is still a developer bring-up: you build it on your Mac from your own IPSW and your own phone's firmware. No Apple software and no vendor firmware are distributed here. [中文說明在下方](#繁體中文)

## Highlights

- 🎧 **AirPods**: open the case and iOS shows its card with Connect; the AirPods Pro 2 pair, and music and the keyboard's clicks play clean.
- 🧈 **Smoother scrolling**: the display controller now scales and flips the picture itself. Late frames while scrolling: 13% → 3–4%.
- 🔵 **Bluetooth pairings stay** across restarts.
- 🔊 **Speaker and microphone** no longer go silent when the camera starts first ([#1](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-fajita/issues/1), thanks to @tre4surehunter9).

## What's new

### Added
- **The AirPods card** (SharingViewService, ProductKit) gets its pictures: `mobileassetd`, denied since the first build, runs, and fetches them from Apple's asset servers (over Wi‑Fi, or the cellular data when there is no Wi‑Fi). The download is a zip that StreamingZip's unzip service unpacks, and it asked the folder's data protection class and gave up on "not supported" (the data volume has no data protection): the kernel now answers class D, "no protection", which is what such a file has. Every MobileAsset zip unpacks now, not only the AirPods'.
- **The display controller scales and flips** (`fb_hw`, on by default). The boot chain leaves the panel's picture on VIG0, the SDM845 display pipe with a QSEED3 scaler, in command mode with auto-refresh, fetching physical memory. A frame is now copied 1:1 (the canvas size, a memcpy) into one of two buffers in the reserved splash region, VIG0 is pointed at it and flushed, and the scaler fits it to the 1080 × 2340 panel, bilinear; the other buffer is written only once the controller has taken the flip, so nothing is torn. Measured with `rc.jobs.smooth6t` (35 scripted gestures, the Dynamic Island on): frames later than 16.7 ms 13% → 3–4%, later than 33 ms 2% → 1%, the copy 2 ms instead of the CPU scaler's 8–11 ms. A panic puts VIG0 back as the boot chain left it, so its text shows. `fb_hw=0` scales on the CPU as before.

### Changed
- **Bluetooth at 3.2 Mbit/s**: the WCN3990's UART ran at 115200 baud, about 90 kbit/s of data, and an AirPods AAC stream is 256 kbit/s: clicks and drop-outs. The chip is told the new speed (Qualcomm's command 0xfc48), the UART's clock goes to 102.4 MHz, and the version is asked again to check it; if the chip does not answer, it is started again at 115200. The phone's own Bluetooth settings file already asks for 3.2 Mbit/s. `bt_baud=115200` keeps it slow.

### Fixed
- **Bluetooth pairings** were forgotten at every start: `containermanagerd` left the `Library` folders of the system's shared containers unreadable for the user `mobile` (mode 000, owner nobody), so `bluetoothd` could not open its device database, nor Maps, the battery history and others theirs. The boot gives them back to `mobile` before anything starts.
- **No sound and no microphone** when the camera took the audio's SMMU context between two of the audio driver's steps ([#1](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-fajita/issues/1)): a context's registers were read through the mapping of the context chosen before, and a context found in use ended the search. Reported, diagnosed and fixed by @tre4surehunter9; their patch is in as they wrote it. The speaker's step 15 also keeps its context for step 17 now, which made the speaker silent in about half of the starts.
- The phone's name set in Settings → General → About is kept (`lockdownd` runs).
- Files and TrollStore's file picker open: their extensions are registered with LaunchServices.
- A panic after long use with many files open: `kern.maxvnodes` is 65536.

## Known issues

- **Every recording after the first of a start is silent** ([#2](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-fajita/issues/2)): fixed in [Beta 0.7.1](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-fajita/releases/tag/v0.7.1-beta).
- **The AirPods card's animation** stays black; the card works. It may say "Not Your AirPods" for AirPods tied to another Apple ID, and Connect pairs them all the same.
- **Videos in Safari** (YouTube) may not play: WebKit's media process is stopped as if Safari were in the background. Looked into next.
- **iOS downloads system assets** now (a few MB to tens of MB). Without Wi‑Fi this goes over the cellular data.
- **Wi‑Fi** does not join an access point that offers only 802.11g (no HT).
- **SMS** received while HakiOS runs are not kept; no calls; no App Store or Apple ID.

# 繁體中文

**HakiOS for OnePlus 6T** 讓 OnePlus 6T 跑 Darwin（XNU）核心＋iOS 16.5 使用者空間。安裝後按電源鍵直接進 iOS，Android 留在另一個 slot。目前仍是開發者移植：用你自己的 IPSW 和你自己手機的韌體在 Mac 上建置，這裡不散布任何 Apple 軟體或原廠韌體。

## 重點

- 🎧 **AirPods**：打開盒蓋，iOS 會跳出卡片和「連線」；AirPods Pro 2 能配對，音樂和鍵盤聲都清楚。
- 🧈 **滑動更順**：畫面改由顯示控制器自己縮放、翻頁。滑動時晚到的幀 13% → 3～4%。
- 🔵 **藍牙配對重開機後會保留**。
- 🔊 **喇叭和麥克風**不會再因為相機先啟動而沒聲音（[#1](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-fajita/issues/1)，感謝 @tre4surehunter9）。

## 更新內容

### 新增
- **AirPods 的卡片**（SharingViewService、ProductKit）拿得到圖了：從第一版起被擋掉的 `mobileassetd` 會跑了，會從 Apple 的素材伺服器下載（有 Wi‑Fi 走 Wi‑Fi，沒有就走行動數據）。下載的是 zip，由 StreamingZip 的解壓服務解開，而它會問資料夾的資料保護等級，拿到「不支援」就放棄（資料分區沒有資料保護）：核心現在回答 D 級「不保護」，這正是這種檔案的實際狀態。所有 MobileAsset 的 zip 現在都解得開，不只 AirPods 的。
- **由顯示控制器縮放、翻頁**（`fb_hw`，預設開）。開機程式把面板的畫面放在 VIG0（SDM845 有 QSEED3 縮放器的那種圖層），命令模式加自動刷新，直接讀實體記憶體。現在每一幀原樣（畫布大小，memcpy）複製進保留的開機畫面區裡兩塊緩衝區之一，把 VIG0 指過去並 flush，由縮放器雙線性縮成 1080 × 2340；另一塊要等控制器接手翻頁後才寫，所以不會撕裂。用 `rc.jobs.smooth6t` 量（35 個自動手勢，開動態島）：晚於 16.7 ms 的幀 13% → 3～4%，晚於 33 ms 的 2% → 1%，複製 2 ms，原本 CPU 縮放要 8～11 ms。當機時會把 VIG0 還原成開機時的設定，錯誤文字才看得到。`fb_hw=0` 回到 CPU 縮放。

### 改進
- **藍牙 3.2 Mbit/s**：WCN3990 的 UART 原本跑 115200 baud，約 90 kbit/s 的資料量，AirPods 的 AAC 串流要 256 kbit/s，所以會破音、斷斷續續。現在用高通的指令（0xfc48）告訴晶片新速度、UART 時脈改成 102.4 MHz，再問一次版本確認；晶片沒回應就重開回 115200。手機原廠的藍牙設定檔本來就是 3.2 Mbit/s。`bt_baud=115200` 可維持低速。

### 修正
- **藍牙配對**每次開機都會忘記：`containermanagerd` 把系統共用容器的 `Library` 資料夾設成使用者 `mobile` 讀不了（權限 000、擁有者 nobody），`bluetoothd` 打不開它的裝置資料庫，地圖、電池紀錄等也一樣。現在開機時、在任何服務啟動前改回給 `mobile`。
- **相機在音訊驅動兩個步驟之間占走 SMMU context 時，喇叭和麥克風全啞**（[#1](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-fajita/issues/1)）：context 的暫存器是透過先前選到的 context 的對應讀的，遇到被占用的 context 也會直接放棄。由 @tre4surehunter9 回報、診斷並修好，他的修補原樣併入。另外喇叭的步驟 15 現在會把 context 留給步驟 17，原本這讓約一半的開機沒聲音。
- 「設定 → 一般 → 關於」改的手機名稱會保留（`lockdownd` 會跑了）。
- 「檔案」和 TrollStore 的選檔畫面能打開：它們的擴充功能已向 LaunchServices 註冊。
- 長時間使用、開很多檔案後的當機：`kern.maxvnodes` 提高到 65536。

## 已知問題

- **開機後第一次以後的錄音都沒聲音**（[#2](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-fajita/issues/2)）：已在 [Beta 0.7.1](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-fajita/releases/tag/v0.7.1-beta) 修正。
- **AirPods 卡片的動畫**是黑的，卡片本身能用。綁在別的 Apple ID 的 AirPods 會顯示「Not Your AirPods」，按「連線」一樣能配對。
- **Safari 裡的影片**（YouTube）可能播不出來：WebKit 的媒體程式被當成 Safari 在背景而停掉。下一步會查。
- **iOS 現在會下載系統素材**（幾 MB 到幾十 MB），沒有 Wi‑Fi 時會用行動數據。
- **Wi‑Fi** 連不上只支援 802.11g（沒有 HT）的基地台。
- **簡訊**：HakiOS 執行時收到的不會保存；不能打電話；不能用 App Store、Apple ID。
