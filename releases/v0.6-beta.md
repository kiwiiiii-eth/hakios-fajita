# Beta 0.6 — the cameras, TrollStore, and a Dynamic Island to choose

**HakiOS for the OnePlus 6T** runs the Darwin (XNU) kernel and the iOS 16.5 user space on a OnePlus 6T. Installed, the phone starts iOS from its power button; Android stays in its other slot. It is still a developer bring-up: you build it on your Mac from your own IPSW and your own phone's firmware. No Apple software and no vendor firmware are distributed here. [中文說明在下方](#繁體中文)

## Highlights

- 📷 **The cameras work.** The Camera app shows the main camera at about 30 frames a second with autofocus and optical image stabilisation, and the front camera; photographs are saved to Photos.
- 📦 **TrollStore 2.1.1 comes installed**: install your own IPAs on the phone.
- 🏝 **A Dynamic Island, if you want one**: `ISLAND=1 ./install.sh` makes iOS draw an iPhone 14 Pro's island; without it, the usual status bar.

## What's new

### Added
- **Main camera** (Sony IMX519, 16 MP): preview at 29.9 frames a second from its binned 2328x1746, the colour from the phone's own tuning (the files `setup.sh` takes from your phone), autofocus (the module's SA3103 lens motor, contrast-measured), and optical image stabilisation (the BU24218 driver, on by default; `EXTRA="cam_ois=0"` leaves it off). A photograph is the sharpest of four frames taken after the shutter. `EXTRA="cam_full=1"` uses the whole 4656x3496 array instead, at about 20 frames a second.
- **Front camera** (Sony IMX371): 2304x1728. It is a Quad Bayer sensor, so only its binned mode is used.
- **TrollStore 2.1.1** (opa334, MIT), installed into an app container at the first start and kept registered; ldid is beside it. `TROLLSTORE=0 ./setup.sh` leaves it out.
- **Dynamic Island**, chosen when installing (`ISLAND=1 ./install.sh`) or for a run from the Mac (`EXTRA="island=1" ./run.sh`): the loader gives the user space an iPhone 14 Pro's artwork subtype, and SpringBoard draws the island. Installing again without `ISLAND=1` brings the status bar back; the system's cached answers are dropped whenever the choice changes.
- `setup.sh` takes the cameras' files (their sensors' tables and colour tuning) from the phone: over adb from Android, or, when Android does not start, read through the loader from the vendor partition (nothing is written; needs `brew install e2fsprogs`).

### Changed
- While the camera runs, the GPU is kept at its fast speed (the screen showed black patches during animations).
- **Photographs are JPEG by default** (Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible), unless a format was chosen before: High Efficiency (HEIF) is encoded on the processors here and takes a few seconds a photograph, a JPEG 0.2 s.
- When the phone gets hot the camera makes fewer frames (every other one from 88 °C, one in three from 95 °C) instead of holding the processors at full speed.

### Fixed
- The front camera's picture was purple (its Quad Bayer pattern was read as an ordinary one).
- The camera knocked at every switch between front and back: the focus lens is now set down first and the stabiliser centred before it sleeps.

## Known issues

- **The Dynamic Island** (`ISLAND=1`) lays the phone out as one with a home button (no gestures, the island over the icons): fixed in [Beta 0.6.1](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-fajita/releases/tag/v0.6.1-beta).
- **The second rear camera** (IMX376K) is not used; no portrait mode or zoom lens.
- **Video** recording saves a movie with sound, but has not been looked at closely yet; reports welcome.
- **High Efficiency** (if you choose it in Settings → Camera → Formats) takes a few seconds a photograph.
- **Black patches** can still flash on the screen during some animations.
- **Wi‑Fi** does not join an access point that offers only 802.11g (no HT): it accepts the association, but the WPA2 handshake never starts. Beta 0.5 behaves the same. Access points with 802.11n or newer join as before.
- **Apple's services** need an activated device and are not supported (App Store, Apple ID, Apple Maps).
- **Bluetooth** pairings are forgotten at every boot; with `./run.sh` nothing on the phone is kept (install for that).
- Scrolling is not as smooth as an iPhone's; no calls or SMS; `neofetch` says "iPhone X / A11".

# 繁體中文

**HakiOS for OnePlus 6T** 讓 OnePlus 6T 跑 Darwin（XNU）核心＋iOS 16.5 使用者空間。安裝後按電源鍵直接進 iOS，Android 留在另一個 slot。目前仍是開發者移植：用你自己的 IPSW 和你自己手機的韌體在 Mac 上建置，這裡不散布任何 Apple 軟體或原廠韌體。

## 重點

- 📷 **相機能用了**：相機 App 的主鏡頭預覽約每秒 30 幀，有自動對焦與光學防手震；前鏡頭也能用；照片存進「照片」。
- 📦 **內建 TrollStore 2.1.1**：可以在手機上安裝自己的 IPA。
- 🏝 **可選動態島**：`ISLAND=1 ./install.sh` 讓 iOS 畫出 iPhone 14 Pro 的動態島；不加就是一般狀態列。

## 更新內容

### 新增
- **主鏡頭**（Sony IMX519，1600 萬畫素）：預覽每秒 29.9 幀（2328x1746 合併像素模式），色彩用手機原廠調校（`setup.sh` 從你的手機抽出的檔案），自動對焦（模組上的 SA3103 對焦馬達，對比式），光學防手震（BU24218，預設開啟；`EXTRA="cam_ois=0"` 關閉）。拍照會取快門後四張中最清楚的一張。`EXTRA="cam_full=1"` 改用完整 4656x3496，約每秒 20 幀。
- **前鏡頭**（Sony IMX371）：2304x1728。它是四合一（Quad Bayer）感光元件，所以只用合併像素模式。
- **TrollStore 2.1.1**（opa334，MIT 授權）：第一次開機時裝進 App 容器並保持註冊，旁邊附 ldid。`TROLLSTORE=0 ./setup.sh` 可不裝。
- **動態島**：安裝時選（`ISLAND=1 ./install.sh`），或從 Mac 開機時加（`EXTRA="island=1" ./run.sh`）。loader 會把機型外觀代號改成 iPhone 14 Pro 的，SpringBoard 就畫出動態島。不加 `ISLAND=1` 再裝一次就回到一般狀態列；切換時系統快取會自動清掉。
- `setup.sh` 會從手機抽相機檔（感光元件設定表與色彩調校）：Android 開得起來就走 adb；開不起來就透過 loader 唯讀讀取 vendor 分區（不寫入任何東西；需要 `brew install e2fsprogs`）。

### 改進
- 相機運作時 GPU 維持高速（動畫時畫面原本會閃黑塊）。
- **照片預設存 JPEG**（「設定 → 相機 → 格式 → 最相容」），之前自己選過格式的維持原選擇：「高效率」（HEIF）在這裡用 CPU 編碼，一張要幾秒，JPEG 只要 0.2 秒。
- 手機過熱時相機少出幀（88 °C 起每兩幀出一幀、95 °C 起每三幀出一幀），不再把 CPU 鎖在最高速。

### 修正
- 前鏡頭畫面偏紫（四合一排列被當成一般排列讀）。
- 前後鏡頭切換時的「喀」聲：現在先把對焦鏡片放下、防手震置中後才休眠。

## 已知問題

- **動態島**（`ISLAND=1`）會被當成有 Home 鍵的機型（沒有手勢、島壓在圖示上）：已在 [Beta 0.6.1](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-fajita/releases/tag/v0.6.1-beta) 修正。
- **第二顆後鏡頭**（IMX376K）還沒用上：沒有人像模式與長焦。
- **錄影**能存出有聲音的影片，但還沒仔細檢查畫質，歡迎回報。
- **高效率格式**（若在「設定 → 相機 → 格式」改選）一張照片要幾秒。
- **黑塊**：部分動畫時畫面仍可能閃黑塊。
- **Wi‑Fi** 連不上只支援 802.11g（沒有 HT）的基地台：連結會被接受，但 WPA2 握手一直沒有開始；Beta 0.5 也一樣。支援 802.11n 以上的基地台照常連得上。
- **Apple 服務**需要已啟用的裝置，不支援（App Store、Apple ID、Apple 地圖）。
- **藍牙**配對每次開機都要重來；用 `./run.sh` 時資料不會保留（要保留請安裝）。
- 滑動還沒有 iPhone 順；不能打電話、傳簡訊；`neofetch` 顯示 iPhone X／A11。
