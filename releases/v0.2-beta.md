# HakiOS on the OnePlus 6T — Beta 0.2 (`v0.2-beta`)

November 2026. Kernel: [`kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-xnu-kernel`](https://github.com/kiwiiiii-eth/hakios-xnu-kernel/tree/fajita-v0.2-beta),
tag `fajita-v0.2-beta`. Device: this repository, tag `v0.2-beta`.

Still a developer bring-up, as Beta 0.1: tethered to a Mac over USB, the whole system in RAM (nothing is flashed; a reboot
goes back to Android), built on your Mac with your own IPSW and your own phone's firmware. No Apple software and no vendor
firmware are distributed here.

## What is new since Beta 0.1

- **The microphone** (on by default): Voice Memos records and plays back. The bottom microphone (AMIC4, with its bias) goes
  through the WCD9340 codec over SLIMbus (the satellite's NGD and BAM driven by the kernel, the bus's master in the ADSP
  asked for over QMI) to the ADSP's AFE and an ASM recording session, and on to Darwin as `/dev/hakios-mic`. The path is up
  only while something records; the speaker and the microphone work at the same time. `adsp8_mic=0` leaves it off.
- **BLE keyboards type**: pair one in Settings → Bluetooth and type. iOS's `BTLEServer` (HID over GATT) now runs with
  Bluetooth, and the kernel's IOHIDResource answers it as IOHIDFamily would (an IOHIDUserDevice for each keyboard, its
  queue and notification port); the keys reach the system as a Bluetooth keyboard's.
- **Ringtones** confirmed on the phone (Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone).
- **Touch** is read 120 times a second on a steady beat (it was about 80, unevenly), and a finger raises every group of
  CPU cores and the GPU to full speed at once.

## Known issues

- Scrolling is not yet smooth: about 50 frames a second, with a third of the frames late; the time goes in the window
  server's drawing on the CPU side and the scaling of each frame onto the panel, not in the GPU.
- Bluetooth pairings are lost at every boot (the system is in RAM); Control Center shows Bluetooth off.
- No cameras, no persistent storage (`/private/var` is made afresh at every boot).
- Wi-Fi networks are joined again by hand after every boot.
- Some routers isolate their clients: if your Mac cannot reach the phone over Wi-Fi, put both on an iPhone's hotspot.
- `neofetch` says "iPhone X / A11": the user space believes it is an iPhone10,3.
- Rarely, a run stops at boot with `initproc failed to start` (programs killed by SIGBUS as the modem starts): run it again.
- `pull-firmware.sh` (firmware over adb) was tried against a stand-in for the phone only: report how it goes.

# 繁體中文

仍是開發者移植，跟 Beta 0.1 一樣：

- 要用 USB 接著 Mac 開機。
- 整套系統跑在記憶體裡，不刷任何分區，重開機就回到 Android。
- 用你自己的 IPSW 和你自己手機的韌體在 Mac 上建置。
- 這裡不散布任何 Apple 軟體或原廠韌體。

**新增：**
- **麥克風**（預設開啟）：語音備忘錄能錄音、播放。
  - 路徑：底部麥克風（AMIC4）→ WCD9340 codec → SLIMbus → ADSP 錄音 → `/dev/hakios-mic`。
  - 只在有程式錄音時才通電；喇叭和麥克風可以同時用。`adsp8_mic=0` 可關閉。
- **BLE 鍵盤能打字**：在「設定 → 藍牙」配對後就能打字。
  - iOS 負責藍牙鍵盤的 `BTLEServer` 現在會跟著藍牙啟動。
  - 核心的 IOHIDResource 補齊 iOS 要的功能，按鍵以藍牙鍵盤的身分送進系統。
- **鈴聲**：已在手機上確認可用。
- **觸控**：每秒穩定讀 120 次（原本約 80 次且不平均）；手指一碰，所有 CPU 組和 GPU 立刻升到全速。

**已知問題：**
- 滑動還不夠順：約每秒 50 幀，三分之一的幀會晚到。時間花在視窗伺服器用 CPU 準備畫面、以及把畫面縮放到面板上，不是 GPU。
- 每次開機藍牙配對都會消失（系統在記憶體裡）；控制中心的藍牙顯示關閉。
- 沒有相機，也不能保存資料（重開機後清空）。
- 每次開機都要重新加入 Wi‑Fi。
- 部分路由器會隔離用戶端。Mac 連不到手機時，讓兩台都連 iPhone 熱點。
- `neofetch` 會顯示 iPhone X／A11，因為系統以為自己是 iPhone X。
- 偶爾開機會停在 `initproc failed to start`：再跑一次即可。
